Questions & Answers

FREQUENTLY
ASKED

How hot does the ARCTHERMA ONE get?

+

The ARCTHERMA ONE reaches temperatures between 40°C and 85°C (104°F–185°F), controlled precisely via the digital thermal management system. We recommend starting at 55°C for first sessions and gradually increasing.

How long does it take to set up?

+

The ARCTHERMA ONE is designed to unfold and be operational in under 7 minutes. The magnetic panel system requires zero tools, and the steam generator reaches optimal temperature in just 4 minutes.

What materials is it made from?

+

The outer shell uses premium arctic-grade thermal insulation fabric — the same material used in high-altitude mountaineering equipment. Interior surfaces are 100% organic, free from BPAs and synthetic chemicals.

Can I use it while travelling?

+

Absolutely. The ARCTHERMA ONE folds into a premium carry bag weighing just 4.8 kg. It operates on both 110V and 220V worldwide — ideal for hotels, Airbnbs, or any space with a standard power outlet.

What’s included in the box?

+

Each ARCTHERMA ONE includes: the sauna tent, digital steam generator, ergonomic folding chair, remote temperature controller, premium carry bag, and a 30-day wellness protocol guide co-designed with Nordic health experts.

What is the warranty policy?

+

Every ARCTHERMA ONE comes with a 2-year comprehensive warranty covering all components. We also offer a 60-day full money-back guarantee — no questions asked, no conditions.
