FAQ

Everything you
need to know.

Traditional saunas heat the air around you to 180–220°F. Infrared saunas heat your body directly using light waves, operating at a more comfortable 110–150°F — yet penetrating 4–5cm into tissue for deeper therapeutic benefit. You sweat more, recover faster, and experience longer sessions without discomfort.
Full spectrum means IGNIS emits Near, Mid, and Far infrared wavelengths simultaneously. Near IR (700–1400nm) penetrates the skin surface for collagen and wound healing. Mid IR (1.4–3μm) targets muscle tissue. Far IR (3–1000μm) goes deepest — reaching organs and triggering detoxification. Most budget saunas only use Far IR. IGNIS uses all three.
The IGNIS Pro draws approximately 1.7kW and typically runs 45–60 minute sessions. At average US electricity rates (~$0.14/kWh), a daily session costs roughly $0.16–$0.22 — less than a cup of coffee. The IGNIS Solo draws even less at 1.1kW.
All IGNIS models require a standard 120V (Solo) or 240V (Pro/Sanctuary) outlet — no special plumbing or ventilation needed. Our white glove team delivers, assembles, and tests your unit in your home. Most installations are complete in 3–5 hours. We leave only when you're completely satisfied.
Yes. Daily use at 30–45 minutes is the protocol used in most of the clinical studies supporting infrared's benefits. We recommend staying well-hydrated before and after sessions. Consult your physician if pregnant or managing specific cardiovascular conditions. Most healthy adults benefit from daily or near-daily sessions.
